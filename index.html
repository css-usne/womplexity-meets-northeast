Womplexity Meets Northeast

Connecting Communities in Complex Systems Science

A satellite session celebrating women researchers across disciplines and career stages

About This Event

Building Networks, Bridging Disciplines

Complex systems science thrives on interdisciplinary exchange and diverse perspectives. Yet women remain underrepresented across many of its subfields, from computational social science and network science to ecological modeling and statistical physics.

This satellite session brings together two complementary community-building efforts: Womplexity (Women in Complexity) and the Complex Systems Society U.S. Northeast Chapter, to create a space where women researchers at all career stages can share their work, find mentorship, and grow professional networks that cross both disciplinary and geographic boundaries.

Our Goal

Create a scientifically rigorous venue where women in complex systems can present their research in a supportive, low-pressure environment that encourages mentorship and lasting professional relationships.

Diversity First

We prioritize diversity in career stage (doctoral students to senior faculty) and disciplinary background, showcasing multiple viable career trajectories in complex systems science.

Event Abstract

While the representation of women in STEM fields has improved over the past decades, progress remains uneven and fragile, particularly in interdisciplinary areas like complex systems, where researchers often fall between the support structures of traditional departments. Institutional policies around hiring and funding have been necessary but insufficient on their own. Parallel efforts at the community level can help prevent isolation and disconnection from peers facing similar challenges.

The satellite is organized as a half-day afternoon program designed to foster both scientific depth and personal exchange. The session will host invited keynote talks with established woman researchers whose career trajectory can serve as inspiration for early-career scientists, complemented by short contributed talks from women across different complex systems subfields.

A dedicated segment introduces the activities and projects of the CSS U.S. Northeast Chapter, offering attendees a concrete entry point into a regional community. The session closes with a panel discussion featuring women working in different areas of complex systems science, structured around three guiding questions addressing themes critical to women's experiences in STEM: building collaborative networks across institutions, navigating career transitions, and how community organizations can best support early-career researchers.

Our Communities

Womplexity

Women in Complexity is an international network fostering career development and mentorship for women in complex systems science. Womplexity creates spaces for collaboration, visibility, and mutual support across the global research community.

  • International network
  • Career mentorship
  • Professional development
  • Research collaboration

CSS/US-NE Chapter

Complex Systems Society U.S. Northeast Chapter is the official chapter of the Complex Systems Society serving the U.S. Northeast region (primarily New England and Middle Atlantic). We organize seminars, reading groups, and collaborative initiatives throughout the year.

  • Regional events & seminars
  • Reading groups
  • Research collaboration
  • Community building
